K8 Study privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

What the app stores

K8 Study has no accounts and no server of its own. Your study progress (notes read, flashcard boxes, quiz scores, streaks and XP) is saved only on your device. We never receive it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Ads

The Android and iOS apps show ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and information about how ads are viewed and tapped. Google's use of this data is covered by its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can reset your device's advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings at any time.

The website version at heemo521.github.io/k8-study shows no ads.

Children

K8 Study is meant for adults studying for Kubernetes certifications and is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
